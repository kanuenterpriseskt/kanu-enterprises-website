Safety & HSE Commitment

Safety Driven Maintenance Services

At Kanu Enterprises, safety is an integral part of all maintenance and manpower activities. We believe that safe execution ensures quality output, operational reliability and long-term client confidence.

Our Safety Practices

  • Work permit compliance and task-specific risk assessment
  • Use of appropriate PPE as per job requirements
  • Pre-job safety briefing and toolbox talks
  • Safe tools, equipment and working methods
  • Housekeeping and work area control

Training & Awareness

Our workforce is oriented on basic safety practices, hazard identification and safe work procedures relevant to industrial maintenance activities. Regular toolbox talks and on-site supervision ensure compliance with safety standards.

Client & Statutory Compliance

We follow client safety rules and statutory requirements applicable to factory and industrial sites. Safety execution is aligned with site-specific procedures and permit systems to ensure disciplined and controlled work practices.
