Our Maintenance Services

Mechanical Maintenance

We provide comprehensive mechanical maintenance services covering preventive, breakdown and condition-based maintenance for rotating and static equipment. Our scope includes pumps, gearboxes, conveyors, compressors, material handling systems and associated mechanical assemblies to ensure safe and reliable operation.

Electrical Maintenance

Our electrical maintenance services include inspection, testing and troubleshooting of motors, control panels, MCCs, power distribution systems and field cabling. We focus on preventive practices to minimize electrical failures and unplanned downtime.

Preventive & Breakdown Maintenance

Planned preventive maintenance to reduce failures and quick response breakdown maintenance to minimize production downtime.

Shutdown Maintenance

We provide planned shutdown maintenance support with structured execution, adequate manpower and strict safety compliance. Our shutdown services focus on job planning, permit coordination, safe execution and timely completion to minimize production loss and ensure smooth plant restart.

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Our Annual Maintenance Contracts are designed to support continuous plant operations through planned maintenance, breakdown response and routine inspections. AMC scope is customized based on plant requirements, equipment criticality and safety standards to ensure long-term operational reliability.

Manpower Supply

We provide skilled and semi-skilled technical manpower to support industrial maintenance and project activities. Our manpower deployment includes fitters, electricians, technicians and helpers, supplied with proper supervision, safety orientation and compliance to site requirements. Manpower supply is managed to ensure productivity, discipline and adherence to factory safety standards.

All deployed manpower is briefed on safety practices and works under defined scope and supervision as per client and statutory requirements.
